# Driftline in-game help ticker
# Pages are separated by a line with: ---

Controls: Arrow keys steer and thrust. Ctrl = bullets. Tab = bombs. Shift+Tab = mines.
Abilities: Home Stealth, Shift+Home Cloak, End XRadar, Shift+End Antiwarp.
Chat: Enter to open. Esc to close. F1 cycles help pages.

---

Energy: HP and resource share the same bar.
Damage reduces energy; weapons/abilities also consume energy.
Energy recharges after a short delay. Watch ENG and the recharge timer (R:).

---

Safe Zone: immune to damage while inside.
Safe Zone time can be limited; HUD shows SZ countdown when enabled.
Map/Radar: learn the arena lanes, doors, and safe-zone geometry.

---

Items: Shift+Ctrl Repel, Shift+Del Burst, Ins Warp, F3 Rocket, F4 Brick.
F5 Decoy, F6 Thor, Shift+Ins Portal.
Pick up green prizes to earn items. Left HUD shows your current stock.

---

Gun levels: L1 single shot. L2 double with bounce. L3 triple with shrapnel.
Bomb levels: L1 basic. L2 heavier. L3 heavy with shrapnel on impact.
MultiFire: fires a three-shot spread. Won by prize, toggled with Del.

---

Afterburner: hold Shift while thrusting for a speed burst (drains energy fast).
Rocket: F3 gives a 5-second max-speed boost when you have rockets.
Portal: Shift+Ins drops a return beacon; press again to warp back to it.

---

Chat commands: ?help lists them. ?ping ?status ?flags ?team ?kill ?lines=N ?ignore
Type =NNNN to change frequency. Macros like %coord and %energy expand as you send.
